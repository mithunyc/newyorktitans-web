# Find your place in the club.

Titans is built around standards: skill, character, discipline, community. We're not looking for perfect cricketers. We're looking for people who care about the game and want to play it the right way.

## What joining means

When you join Titans, you agree to our [Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct). That means treating opponents, officials, and teammates with respect at all times — in matches, at training, and online.

You also commit to showing up prepared, communicating when you can't, and investing in the team alongside your own development.

## What Titans offers you

- Competitive cricket at a club that takes both the game and its people seriously.
- Coaching and development that focuses on the whole player, not just statistics.
- A team of people from across New York who share your values.
- A club that will still be here in ten years, built to last.

## Three ways to get involved

**As a player.** Whether you're coming back to cricket after a break or looking for a club that matches your standards, tell us about yourself.

**As a coach or mentor.** If you have knowledge to share — technical, tactical, or life experience — we want to hear how you'd like to contribute.

**As a volunteer or supporter.** From match-day logistics to social media to administrative support, the club runs because people choose to help.
