# A home for cricket in New York.

New York Titans is a nonprofit and community-first cricket organization. We were built with a clear purpose: to develop talented players, strengthen families, and create a lasting institution for cricket, leadership, and community in New York.

## Mission

To use the standards of cricket — skill, fairness, respect, courage — to develop players, build belonging, and earn the credibility to become a serious civic institution for the game in New York.

## Vision

A city where cricket is more than recreation. A city where Titans is the club families recommend, players aspire to, and partners are proud to support.

## Values

- **Integrity** — what we do when no one is watching.
- **Respect** — for opponents, officials, teammates, and the game.
- **Teamwork** — the foundation of every result we are proud of.
- **Discipline** — the daily standard that turns ambition into outcome.
- **Community** — we serve people, not just match days.
- **Enjoyment** — the reason any of this is worth doing.

## Community

Cricket can do more than entertain. It can mentor, develop leadership, create belonging, and bring families together. Titans is built around three commitments to the city we play in:

- **Youth development.** We invest in the next generation of players through pathways, coaching, and mentorship.
- **Belonging.** We make room for people from every background that calls New York home.
- **Leadership through sport.** We use the game to develop character, responsibility, and confidence in players who carry it back into their lives off the field.

## Standards

We hold every member of the club — players, coaches, parents, and supporters — to a written standard of conduct. [Read our Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct) for the full standard.
