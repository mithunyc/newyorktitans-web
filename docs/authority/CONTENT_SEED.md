# CONTENT_SEED.md

## Purpose

This document is the starter copy and content seed for the New York Titans website.

It is not a final marketing bible. It is a strong, grounded first-pass content system designed to help the site launch without filler, hype, or AI-slop language.

Use this document to populate the initial website. Tighten, localize, and replace placeholder details as real team information, sponsor information, and photo assets become available.

---

## Tone Rules

All copy should feel:

- proud
- disciplined
- warm
- credible
- community-rooted
- human
- concise

All copy must avoid:

- inflated claims
- empty hype
- generic sports clichés
- corporate nonprofit jargon
- artificial “future of cricket” language
- sounding like a startup pitch deck

---

New York Titans is building more than a cricket team.

## About Page Copy Seed

### About Hero Heading

**About New York Titans**

### About Hero Subcopy

We are building a cricket club rooted in standards, sportsmanship, leadership, and community pride.

### Organization Summary

New York Titans is a nonprofit/community-first cricket organization in New York focused on developing players, strengthening character, and creating a place of belonging through the game of cricket. We believe sport can shape people as much as it shapes results.

### Mission

To provide the community an opportunity and an environment that allow cricketers to realize their full potential; to foster youth, sportsmanship, excellence, and professionalism on the field and in the community; and to promote cricket in the New York region through our club values.

### Vision

To be the most successful cricket club in the New York region, measured not only by results, but by standards, impact, and the strength of the community we build.

### Values Intro

Our values are not decoration. They define how we compete, how we treat people, and what it means to represent Titans.

### Values Block

**Integrity**
We act honestly, fairly, and in the true spirit of the game.

**Respect**
We respect teammates, opponents, umpires, supporters, and the traditions of cricket.

**Teamwork**
We play selflessly and rely on one another on and off the field.

**Discipline**
We work hard, hold standards, and compete with accountability.

**Community**
We are committed to being a positive and welcoming force in the communities we serve.

**Enjoyment**
We believe sport should build joy, confidence, health, and shared pride.

### Standards / Conduct Summary

Representing New York Titans means representing more than yourself. It means carrying the club’s values in competition, training, social settings, and the broader community.

### About CTA

Learn how to join, support, or partner with Titans.

## Placeholder Content Guidance

If real content is not yet available, use these rules:

- use fewer sections, not filler sections
- use fewer images, not weak images
- never invent achievements, numbers, or partnerships
- never use stock copy that could belong to any club
- if a section lacks truthful substance, cut it until real content exists

## Final Rule

The strongest launch copy will feel specific, proud, disciplined, and grounded.

If a line sounds like it could belong equally to a random startup, a generic sports template, or an AI-generated nonprofit landing page, reject it.
